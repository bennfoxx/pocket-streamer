Electrical, Wiring & Soldering Audit (15.9 mm Z-Cavity)

  • Desolder All Pin Headers: Remove factory yellow/black 2.54 mm headers from Front 1×4, Right-Side 1×4, KY-023, MicroSD, and GY-PCM5102; direct-solder 30 AWG silicone wire flush to pads so harnesses stay within 15.9 mm Z.
  • V713 5V Rail Isolation: Side K1 (B1) at topmost right edge (Y = 54.3 mm) wires ONLY to V713 K pad (1-click ON / 2-click OFF, <30µA standby), isolating GY-PCM5102, KCX_BT_EMITTER & PAM8302A from ESP32 sleep current.
  • 3.3V-Only MicroSD & Speaker/Mic Mute: WWZMDiB Mini 3.3V MicroSD has no LDO — connect strictly to regulated 3.3V (never 5V!). Add 100kΩ pull-down on INMP441 SD (GPIO 15); drive PAM8302A SD LOW during B8 PTT so mic & speaker never feedback.
  • LiPo & RF Protection: 503759 LiPo (Z: 1.8..6.8) sits under a 0.8 mm printed PLA-CF shield (Z: 7.0..7.8) with Kapton tape over solder joints; keep ESP32 ceramic antenna wing (X: 54..64) clear of copper pours.
