## Split-Deck `18650` Architecture (`108 × 68 × 34 mm`, ~`172 g`) & Power Safety

Instead of stacking the `21 mm` thick `18650 Shield V3` directly behind the `WM8960` HAT (which creates a clunky `~46 mm` brick), the rear enclosure splits into **two side-by-side vertical bays**:
- **Upper-Rear Bay (`y = -15 mm`):** `WM8960 Hi-Fi HAT` (`65 × 30 mm`) keeps both silver MEMS mics and the `3.5 mm` headphone jack aligned along the top bevel.
- **Lower-Rear Bay (`y = +16.5 mm`):** `ACEIRMC 18650 Shield V3` (`99 × 29.2 × 21 mm`) sits below the `WM8960` (`30 + 29.2 = 59.2 mm` total height inside a `68 mm` tall body — keeping the handheld a slim `34 mm` deep!).

> **CRITICAL Battery Wiring & Flashing Rules (Zero-Solder Safe):**
> 1. **Flip the V3 Switch to `Hold`:** Classic `18650 Shield V3` boards include a `Normal / Hold` slide switch. Use **`Hold`** so low-current idle pauses don't trigger auto-shutoff (`<~50 mA` cutoff in `Normal`).
> 2. **Slide V3 `OFF` Before Plugging USB-C into Your Computer:** Never power the ESP32 `5V` rail from the `18650 Shield V3` at the exact same moment you plug USB-C into a laptop for code flashing — always slide the V3 switch **`OFF`** first!
> 3. **Feed Regulated `5V` Only (Not Dual `3.3V` Rails):** Route the V3 `5V/2A` output (via external short USB-A -> USB-C lead, or internal `B0BV9K69M9` USB-to-DuPont adapter) into **`WM8960 Pin 4 (5V)` & `Pin 39 (GND)`** so the `ideaspark ESP32` generates its own clean `3.3V` logic rail without fighting the shield's `3V/1A` regulator.
> 4. **Verify Polarity Twice Before Clicking Cell In:** `18650 Shield V3` springs have clear `+ / -` molded icons. Double-check flat-top `+` orientation before seating the cell — reverse insertion instantly blows the TP4056/DW01 protection IC.

## Audiobookshelf (`ABS`) + Offline MicroSD Download Workflow

Your **100% ESP32 + MicroSD SPI Reader** hardware supports browsing Audiobookshelf libraries over Wi-Fi, caching upcoming chapters locally to MicroSD (`GPIO 5/18/19/23`), playing offline anywhere via the `18650` battery, and syncing timestamps back when reconnected:
- **REST API Endpoints (Bearer Token Auth — `Authorization: Bearer <your_api_token>`):**
  - `GET /api/items/{id}?include=progress` — fetches book metadata, cover image URL (rendered in `65K` color on your `1.9"` IPS!), track filenames, and last saved position.
  - `GET /api/me/progress/{libraryItemId}` & `PATCH /api/me/progress/{libraryItemId}` — restores and updates live Wi-Fi listening timestamps (`currentTime`, `duration`, `isFinished`).
  - `POST /api/me/sync-local-progress` — uploads queued offline listening sessions stored on MicroSD once Wi-Fi reconnects!
- **`ESP32-WROOM-32` (`520 KB` Internal SRAM, No PSRAM) Audio Format Tip:**
  - Modern `ESP32-audioI2S >= v3.0.0` requires PSRAM (`~700 KB` heap OOMs on non-PSRAM WROOM-32), and heavy multi-hour `.m4b` AAC seek tables strain internal SRAM.
  - Inside **Audiobookshelf (`Settings` / Book -> `Tools` -> `Audio Converter`)**, batch-convert downloaded offline books into **`64 kbps MP3` chapters** (`~29 MB/hour`, fast seek table, zero OOM crashes, instant MicroSD VSPI reads).
